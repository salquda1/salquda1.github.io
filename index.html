Research

Engineering education

I study belonging and persistence among engineering undergraduates and pathways for engineers to teach in K–12 schools.

Applied ergonomics & human factors

My projects include a surgical body support system and a patented guide for placing distal screws in orthopedic implants.

Continuous improvement

I apply lean methods to manufacturing education and healthcare workflows, including visual management in outpatient clinics.

Teaching

I teach manufacturing engineering at Western. In Manufacturing Process Planning, students work with industry partners on ergonomics and lean manufacturing projects. I also mentor undergraduate researchers working on orthopedic device design and hands-on lean manufacturing labs.
